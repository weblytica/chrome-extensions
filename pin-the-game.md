# Privacy Policy — Pin the Game

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Pin the Game is a Chrome extension that shows a small live scoreboard for a sports game you choose.

## What the extension collects

Nothing. Pin the Game has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not collect,
transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

## What the extension stores on your computer

Using Chrome's extension storage, on your device only:

- Your watchlist of teams
- Your last selected level, sport, view filter, and theme
- A cache of college conference names so they load faster

You can clear all of this by removing the extension, or by clearing the extension's site data in
Chrome's settings.

## Network requests

To show scores, the extension requests public, unauthenticated scoreboard data from a sports data
service. These requests contain the sport and league being viewed and nothing about you. The
extension sends no identifiers, no cookies of its own, and no personal data. The data service is
operated by a third party and has its own terms and privacy practices.

## Permissions

The extension requests one Chrome permission, `storage`, used only for the items listed above.
It requests no access to your browsing history, open tabs, or the content of websites.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
